# 💬 Customer Review Sentiment Analysis

## 📌 Introduction

**Customer Review Sentiment Analysis** is a Natural Language Processing (NLP) project that analyzes customer reviews and identifies whether they express a **positive or negative sentiment**.

The project uses a **Kaggle review dataset** and applies text preprocessing and sentiment analysis techniques to understand customer opinions.

The main purpose is to convert large amounts of customer feedback into useful insights that can help understand what customers like and dislike.

---

## 🎯 Project Theme

### **"Understanding Customer Opinions Through Data"**

Customer reviews contain valuable information about their experiences.

This project analyzes those reviews to answer questions such as:

* What percentage of customers are positive?
* What are customers complaining about?
* What do customers like the most?
* Which reviews are positive?
* Which reviews are negative?

## 🧠 Sentiment Analysis

The project classifies customer reviews into sentiment categories.

### 😊 Positive

Reviews expressing satisfaction, appreciation, or a good experience.

Example:

> "The product quality is excellent and I am very happy with my purchase."

### 😞 Negative

Reviews expressing dissatisfaction, complaints, or a poor experience.

Example:

> "The product stopped working after a few days and the quality is disappointing."
